❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which model should I use for a simple FAQ chatbot?

NOVA-100M-new is the best choice due to its superior latency and efficiency for simple text generation.

Can I run llama-2-13b-rlmpq-aggressive on a laptop?

Generally no, unless your laptop is equipped with a high-end workstation GPU. It is designed for server-grade hardware.

Is circuit-breaker worth the extra wait time?

Yes, if your application requires higher reasoning accuracy than a smaller 100M model can provide.
