Deterministic Seed Control

Flux 2 locks latent space geometry tighter than previous versions, turning the seed from a suggestion into an anchor point. By fixing a seed, users lock the composition and structural anatomy, allowing them to swap prompts entirely (e.g., changing a mecha suit to a school uniform) while keeping the pose and coordinates rigid.

Workflow Efficiency & ControlNet Reduction

The high stability of the seed eliminates the need for heavy ControlNet stacks for simple static poses. This saves significant VRAM, allowing for batch processing and the generation of production-ready asset sheets with consistent facial geometry and lighting without manual inpainting.
