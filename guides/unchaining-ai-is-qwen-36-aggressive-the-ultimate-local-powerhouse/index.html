💡 Pro Tips

Pro Tip: Use Q4_K_M quantization for the best balance between speed and model intelligence if you are nearing your VRAM limit.
Pro Tip: Monitor your GPU utilization during inference to see the A3B architecture 'decoupling' during simpler tasks.
Pro Tip: Keep your system drivers updated to the latest version to ensure optimal CUDA/ROCm performance for the Qwen architecture.

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Watch Out: VRAM OOM (Out of Memory): Attempting to run a high-quantization version of the 35B model without enough dedicated VRAM will force offloading to system RAM, drastically slowing performance.
Watch Out: Incorrect Context Length: Setting an excessively large context window can lead to memory fragmentation; keep it within the 4k-8k range initially.

What does 'Aggressive' mean in the model name?

It refers to the model's tuning towards following instructions without hesitation, prioritizing raw capability over 'helpful assistant' conversational filler.

Yes, the model has been fine-tuned to remove safety filters, meaning it will respond to prompts that standard models would otherwise refuse.
