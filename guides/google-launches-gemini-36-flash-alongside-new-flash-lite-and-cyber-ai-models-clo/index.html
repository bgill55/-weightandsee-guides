⚙️ Technical Background

Gemini 3.6 Flash leverages a tiered architecture to balance speed and cost. The 'Flash' variant is optimized for reasoning and multi-turn dialogue, while 'Flash Lite' utilizes quantization or architectural pruning to handle simple, high-throughput tasks. The 'Cyber AI' extension integrates specialized security patterns directly into the model's inference layer, allowing it to detect semantic logic flaws rather than just syntactic errors. The architecture emphasizes low Time to First Token (TTFT) to facilitate real-time conversational agents.
