💡 Pro Tips

Pro Tip: Use 4-bit or 8-bit quantization if your GPU VRAM is limited to ensure the 12B model fits comfortably without offloading to system RAM.
Pro Tip: Implement local caching for frequent prompts to reduce redundant compute cycles.
Pro Tip: Monitor GPU temperature and power draw using 'nvidia-smi' to optimize your local inference environment for sustained performance.

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Watch Out: VRAM Bottleneck: Attempting to run the full precision (FP16) model on cards with less than 16GB of VRAM will cause massive latency spikes.
Watch Out: Ignoring Environment Latency: Failing to account for local Python overhead can make local models appear slower than they actually are during initial cold starts.
