❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI sovereignty and why is it important?

AI sovereignty refers to the ability to maintain control over your AI models and data. It is important because it allows you to run your models as desired, without relying on cloud providers and their potential policy shifts.

What are the benefits of running AI models on local hardware?

Running AI models on local hardware provides architectural independence, optimized performance, and improved security.

What are the system requirements for running AI models on local hardware?

The system requirements for running AI models on local hardware vary depending on the specific model and framework. However, a desktop workstation with a powerful GPU and sufficient RAM is recommended.
