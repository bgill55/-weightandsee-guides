Creating Mac Apps with AI: A Step-by-Step Guide

This guide covers creating personalized Mac apps instantly with AI using Ironsmith, a free, open-source macOS menu bar app. It empowers users to generate, build, and save native SwiftUI apps without requiring coding knowledge or Xcode.
