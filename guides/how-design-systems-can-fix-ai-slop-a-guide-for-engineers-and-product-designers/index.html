📝 Step-by-Step Guide

1

Define Your Style Guide

Create a comprehensive style guide outlining all UI components and their styles. This will serve as the foundation for consistency across your application.

2

Use Design Tokens

Define design tokens (e.g., colors, typography) to ensure consistency in styling. This can be done using a simple one-page file with properties pasted into every session.

color: #3498db;
font-family: 'Arial', sans-serif;
corner-radius: 4px;
3

Cap Your Palette

Limit your color palette to three colors: one accent and two neutrals, with specific hex values. Forbid other colors to achieve a cohesive look.

accent-color: #3498db;
neutral-color1: #ecf0f1;
neutral-color2: #7f8c8d;
4

Write Tokens Down

Document all design tokens in a clear, readable file. This helps maintain consistency across different development sessions.

colors:
  primary: '#3498db'
  secondary: '#ecf0f1'
typography:
  font-family: 'Arial', sans-serif
  font-size: 16px
5

Use Real Content

Replace placeholder text with actual content to prevent the AI from focusing on layout problems and design elements.

real-content: "Lorem ipsum dolor sit amet, consectetur adipiscing elit. Sed do eiusmod tempor incididunt ut labore et dolore magna aliqua."

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Watch Out: Overlooking the importance of consistent styling can lead to a generic, unprofessional look.
Watch Out: Failing to implement design tokens consistently can result in varied styles across different parts of the application.
Watch Out: Using placeholder text instead of real content may not achieve the desired output quality from AI models.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is a design system?

A design system is a set of reusable guidelines that define the look and feel of your software application, ensuring consistency across all parts.

How do I create design tokens?

Design tokens are defined in a clear file specifying colors, typography, button styles, etc., which can be easily referenced and reused throughout your project.

Why is it important to cap my color palette?

Limiting your color palette helps maintain consistency in the design by preventing too many varied colors that could make the interface look messy.

What are some best practices for using real content with AI models?

Replace placeholder text with actual content to guide the AI model towards more distinctive and professional-looking designs.

Can design systems be implemented in any project type?

Yes, design systems can be implemented in various projects, from web applications to mobile apps, to ensure consistency and professionalism in the final product.
