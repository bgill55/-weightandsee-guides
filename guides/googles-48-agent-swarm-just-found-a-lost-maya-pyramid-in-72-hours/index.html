⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Watch Out: Pitfall 1: Over-reliance on raw density - Without rigorous noise filtering, vegetation and terrain noise will generate false positives, overwhelming the agent swarm with irrelevant data.
Watch Out: Pitfall 2: Insufficient parallelization - If the data sectors are not balanced, some agents will finish early while others are bottlenecked, negating the 72-hour speed advantage.
Watch Out: Pitfall 3: Lack of geometric specificity - General anomaly detection is not enough; agents must be configured to look for specific geometric signatures (symmetry, planar faces) associated with Maya architecture.
