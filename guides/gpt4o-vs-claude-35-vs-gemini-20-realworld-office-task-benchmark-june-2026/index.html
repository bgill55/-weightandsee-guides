Step 1: Install Ollama and Configure Llama 4 Turbo

💡 Pro Tips

Pro Tip: Utilize 8-bit quantization (Quant-8) to fit larger models into consumer VRAM without significant performance degradation.
Pro Tip: Monitor thermal throttling on the RTX 6090 during sustained inference to ensure the 600W motor performs at peak efficiency.
Pro Tip: Batch similar tasks (e.g., multiple emails) together to maximize throughput and minimize context-switching overhead.

⚠️ Common Pitfalls

Watch Out: Insufficient VRAM: Attempting to run full-precision models on consumer cards will result in out-of-memory errors; always use quantization.
Watch Out: Thermal Throttling: High-end cards like the RTX 6090 generate significant heat; ensure your case cooling is adequate to maintain clock speeds.
Watch Out: Network Latency: When testing against cloud instances, account for network overhead which can artificially inflate cloud latency figures.

Is local inference really cheaper than cloud computing?

Yes, the benchmark shows a cost reduction of over 90%, with electricity costs averaging roughly $0.01 per 1,000 inferences compared to cloud billing meters.

What is the benefit of 8-bit quantization?

8-bit quantization compresses the model size, allowing it to fit entirely within the GPU's VRAM (like the RTX 6090) while maintaining high inference speed and output quality.
