Step 2: Configure Mesh Density Settings

Locate the mesh density slider within the Hi3D settings panel. Adjust this value based on your specific use case to balance print time against structural integrity.

// Standard Functional Parts
mesh_density = "0.2mm"

// High-Detail Showcase Models
mesh_density = "0.1mm"
3

Step 3: Generate and Slice

Input your text prompt to generate the model. Hi3D will automatically handle boolean operations and support generation. Once the model is generated, proceed directly to slicing as the mesh is already optimized and watertight.

Output: Single-pass topology and texture ready for slicing.

💡 Pro Tips

Pro Tip: Use the 0.2mm mesh density setting for most functional prints to save time without sacrificing strength.
Pro Tip: For artistic or miniature prints, switch to 0.1mm to capture the high-resolution texture synthesis.
Pro Tip: Rely on Hi3D's automated support generation to avoid manual editing of complex overhangs.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Why is Hi3D AI faster than Llama 4?

Hi3D AI generates topology and texture in a single pass, whereas Llama 4 builds geometry first and relies on a secondary network for surface details.

Does Hi3D AI require manual support generation?

No, the Hi3D system handles boolean operations and support generation automatically.

What is the recommended mesh density setting?

Use 0.2 millimeters for standard functional parts to balance speed and integrity, or 0.1 millimeters for high-detail showcase models.
